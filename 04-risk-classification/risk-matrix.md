# AI Risk Classification

Assess dimensions:

- Decision impact
- Financial impact
- Customer impact
- Employee impact
- Data sensitivity
- Autonomy
- Reversibility
- External communication
- Security impact
- Regulatory dependency

## Example Levels

### Low
Internal drafting, low impact, human review.

### Medium
Operational recommendation or workflow assistance.

### High
Material business decisions, sensitive data, consequential external actions.

### Critical
Autonomous or near-autonomous actions with high financial, security, legal, or safety impact.

This is an illustrative governance model, not a legal classification.
