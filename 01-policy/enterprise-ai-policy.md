# Enterprise AI Policy Framework

## Scope

Applies to:
- Generative AI
- Copilots
- AI agents
- Predictive models
- Retrieval-augmented systems
- AI-enabled automation
- Third-party AI services

## Minimum Requirements

Every production use case should define:
- Business owner
- Technical owner
- Purpose
- Users
- Data classes
- Model/provider
- Risk level
- Human oversight
- Evaluation evidence
- Approval status
- Monitoring plan

## Prohibited Practices

- Unapproved exposure of confidential data
- Secret/credential disclosure
- Autonomous high-impact actions without controls
- Hiding material uncertainty
- Using unverified output as authoritative fact
- Bypassing access controls
